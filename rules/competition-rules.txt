HOW TO WIN
----------
The city with the HIGHEST PERCENTAGE GAIN above their baseline wins.

Baseline = 90-day median sale price, frozen 72 h before start.

Formula: ((Highest Sale Price - Baseline) / Baseline) × 100

Example: If your city's median sale price is $300K and you get a $600K sale,
your score is +100% (shown as ×2.0 multiplier)

$300 K baseline → $600 K sale = 200 % of baseline (a +100 % gain, shown in-app as “×2.0”)


SCORING RULES
-------------
1. ONLY SALES DURING THE 14-DAY COMPETITION COUNT
   - Sales before June 23 6AM don't count
   - Sales after July 6 midnight don't count

2. HIGHEST SALE ONLY
   - Only your city's highest sale price matters
   - Lower sales are ignored
   - Your score can only go UP, never down

3. TIE-BREAKER
   - If two cities have the same percentage gain
   - The EARLIER sale wins (by closing date)

4. BASELINE CALCULATION
   - Each city gets a baseline (90-day median price)
   - Baselines are locked before competition starts
   - All cities judged fairly against their own market


WHAT COUNTS AS A SALE

• Must close within competition dates
